# Opções pra seção do urna

O título `# urna`, a imagem e o install (npm e cargo) ficam iguais em todas.

---

## 1

A light binary vector db to carry with you.

Runtime with mmap, HNSW and BM25 indices, SHA hash checks and native AVX2/NEON optimization. It comes with a very simple CLI and TUI, basically: `build`, `ask` and `retrieve`.

Fact-check experiment: [7 public pt-BR fake news datasets](https://github.com/brennercruvinel/fakenews-ptbr-urna-benchmark) ([Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/brennercruvinel/fakenews-ptbr-urna-benchmark)), deduplicated into a 23k-document corpus, with 2.6k queries to measure how well search works.

Image compression experiment with AV1: [38k Magic cards](https://github.com/brennercruvinel/mtg-urna-benchmark) ([Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/brennercruvinel/mtg-urna-benchmark)) turned into video inside the file.
